NOTE ABOUT EDGELINES:
Certain materials have edgelines enabled even when they're completely transparent. The edgelines are also transparent, but they can still inferfere with certain effects. If it's a problem, hiding the material with MME's Subset-Extract function prevents the edgelines from interfering.

NOTE ABOUT NON-REMOVABLE ARTICLES OF CLOTHING:
Certain minor articles of clothing, such as IA's ring, don't have sliders to remove them. However, they can still be removed with subset-extract.
